B5 — About

Where strategy rules.

Dire Battle is a Dota 2 tournament series run by Direborn to find and grow the next generation of Indonesian pro players.

Every team starts in the open. The Open Series feeds the Dire Battle Cup, and the Cup feeds the stages above it. Results decide who moves up.
